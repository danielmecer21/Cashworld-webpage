CASH WORLD WEBSITE - SETUP GUIDE
================================

Everything works right now in DEMO MODE (nothing to set up). In demo mode,
reviews are saved only in the browser you test in, so real customers cannot
see each other's reviews yet. To go live, do PART A (reviews) and PART B (ads).
You only ever edit one file: js/config.js

------------------------------------------------------------
TRY IT FIRST (demo mode)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open reviews.html in your browser, send a test review.
2. Open admin.html in the SAME browser. You will see it waiting.
3. Press Publish, write a reply, then go back to reviews.html.
(Demo mode has no login. Do not upload the site for real use without Part A.)

------------------------------------------------------------
PART A - REAL REVIEWS (Firebase, free plan is enough)
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to https://console.firebase.google.com and sign in with a Google account.
   Click "Create a project" and follow the steps (you can switch Analytics off).

2. In the project, click the "</>" (Web) icon to add a web app. Give it any name.
   Firebase shows a block of code with: apiKey, authDomain, projectId, appId.
   Copy those 4 values into js/config.js, between the quote marks.

3. Left menu > Build > Firestore Database > Create database.
   Choose "Start in production mode", pick a location near you, then Enable.

4. Left menu > Build > Authentication > Get started > Sign-in method >
   Email/Password > Enable > Save.

5. Authentication > Users tab > Add user. Type YOUR admin email and a strong
   password. This is how you will sign in to admin.html.
   After it is created, copy the "User UID" shown in the list.

6. Open the file firestore.rules in this folder. Replace PUT_ADMIN_UID_HERE
   with the User UID you copied. Then in Firebase go to
   Firestore Database > Rules, paste the whole file, and press Publish.
   (Until you do this, nobody can read reviews, which is the safe default.)

7. Authentication > Settings > Authorized domains > Add your real website
   domain (for example cashworld.co.zw). Without this, sign-in fails online.

8. Upload the site. Open yourdomain/admin.html, sign in with the email and
   password from step 5. Reviews customers send appear here as "Waiting".
   You decide what to Publish. You can reply to, hide or delete any review.

Notes
- New reviews are never public until you press Publish.
- admin.html is hidden from search engines and is not linked in the menu, but
  the password is what really protects it.
- Keep the admin password private. If you lose it, use "Add user" again or
  reset it in Firebase > Authentication.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART B - GOOGLE ADS (AdSense)
------------------------------------------------------------
Google decides whether to approve a site. This site is built to meet the
technical needs (privacy policy page, ads.txt, an ad-consent notice, clearly
labelled ad boxes), but approval is not guaranteed. It usually needs a real
domain you own (not a free test address), plenty of your own original content,
and a few weeks of normal traffic.

1. Sign up at https://adsense.google.com and add your website address.

2. AdSense gives you a Publisher ID like  ca-pub-1234567890123456.
   Put it in js/config.js  (adsense > client).

3. For the home page only, open index.html and replace
   ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX  in the line  <meta name="google-adsense-account" ...>
   with your Publisher ID. (Dreamweaver: Edit > Find and Replace.)

4. Open ads.txt. AdSense shows you the exact line to use
   (AdSense > Sites > ads.txt). Paste it in, delete the leading "#".
   ads.txt must sit next to index.html on your website.

5. Upload everything and ask AdSense to review the site.

6. After approval, either:
   a) turn on Auto ads in AdSense (nothing else to do), or
   b) AdSense > Ads > By ad unit > create a "Display ad", copy its number
      (the data-ad-slot value) into js/config.js (adsense > slots > inline).
      Boxes marked "Advertisement" then fill in on 5 pages.

How ads behave on this site
- Visitors see a small notice and ads load only after they press Accept.
- There are no ads on the Contact page or the admin page.
- Never click your own ads, and never ask others to. Google can ban the account.
- Visitors from Europe/UK: Google requires its own consent message for them.
  In AdSense go to Privacy & messaging and turn on the free European
  regulations message.
- Please read privacy.html and check it matches how you run the business.
  It is a sensible starting point, not legal advice.
