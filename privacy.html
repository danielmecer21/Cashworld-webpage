Privacy policy

How Cash World handles information on this website. Last updated October 2026.

Who we are

Cash World is a bureau de change and money transfer business in Zimbabwe. This website, cashworld.co.zw, gives information about our services and branches.

What we collect

  • Reviews. If you send a review we store the name you type, your star rating, your comment and the date. We may publish it, and we may publish a reply to it. Please do not put phone numbers, ID numbers or other private details in a review.
  • Contact form. The contact form does not send anything to our servers. It opens WhatsApp with your message so you can send it to us yourself. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies from that point.
  • Calculator. The currency calculator runs in your browser. We do not save what you type.
  • Your settings. We save your light or dark mode choice and your advertising choice in your browser.

Where reviews are stored

Reviews are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a service provided by Google. Only our administrator can see unpublished reviews or remove reviews.

Advertising

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Ads load only if you press "Accept" on our notice. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and turn off personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Your rights

You can ask us to remove a review you sent, or to tell you what we hold about you, by contacting us below.
